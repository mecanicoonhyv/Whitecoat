Branding and Visual Direction

Tone and Mood
- Celebratory, dignified, warm. Reflects achievement and community.

Typography
- Headings: Playfair Display (serif)
- Body/UI: Inter (sans-serif)
- Fallbacks: system-ui stack

Color Palette (example)
- Primary: #0E7490 (teal 600)
- Accent: #F59E0B (amber 500)
- Neutrals: #111827 (gray 900), #374151 (gray 700), #F3F4F6 (gray 100), white
- Success: #10B981, Warning: #F59E0B, Danger: #EF4444

Imagery
- Use high-quality photos with natural lighting; highlight faces and key moments
- Subtle overlays/gradients for hero to ensure text contrast

Components and Motion
- Rounded corners (md), modest shadows
- Reduce motion preferences supported; non-essential animations minimized

Assets
- Favicon and app icons
- OG images for public pages (Landing, About)

Notes
- Final palette may adapt to institution brand guidelines if provided
